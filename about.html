SHAIKON Engineering Company is a worldwide enterprise specializing in technology development, valve manufacturing, and trading, with a comprehensive integration of R&D, production, marketing, and service. The company's headquarters is situated in Mumbai, India. SHAIKON has obtained certifications for ISO9001, ISO14001, and ISO45001, and collaborates with internationally recognized TPI agencies based on customer needs.

The products offered by the company are extensively utilized in sectors such as petroleum, natural gas, chemicals, and electric power, and are exported worldwide, fostering a strong connection that enables us to serve our customers more efficiently.

Established with the aim of delivering solutions and prioritizing customer service as the foundation of our management philosophy, we strive to enhance the core competitiveness of our products.
