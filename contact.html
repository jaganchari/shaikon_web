EPC Procurement & Compliance FAQ

menu_book STANDARD OPERATING PROTOCOLS (SOP-2025)
01

Which material test certificates (MTC) accompany valve deliveries?

expand_more
Every shipment strictly includes EN 10204 Type 3.1 inspection certification indicating exact heat lot analysis, chemical composition, mechanical tensile testing, Charpy V-notch impact values, and hardness data. EN 10204 Type 3.2 co-certified by designated TPI authorities is provided whenever stipulated in project requisition datasheets.
02

Can Third Party Inspection (TPI) agencies inspect testing on-site?

expand_more
Yes. Our testing bays in Mumbai regularly host client-appointed inspection bodies including Lloyd's Register, Bureau Veritas, DNV GL, TÜV Rheinland, and SGS. We facilitate Witness Testing for API 598 hydrostatic, low-pressure gas seat, Fugitive Emission ISO 15848-1, and Cryogenic functional testing at -196°C.
03

What are the standard manufacturing lead times for exotic alloy trims?

expand_more
Standard ASME carbon and low-temp carbon steels (A105, LF2) ship within 4–6 weeks from pre-machined forged blanks. Exotic alloys such as Inconel 625, Monel 400, Hastelloy C276, and Super Duplex UNS S32750 have an average turnaround of 10–14 weeks, subject to third-party NDT (Radiography, Ultrasonic, Dye Penetrant) mandates.
04

Does SHAIKON support customized actuator integration and controls?

expand_more
Yes. We design and assemble complete automated valve packages fitted with pneumatic scotch-yoke, electro-hydraulic, or multi-turn electric actuators (Rotork, Auma, Bettis), integrating SIL-3 rated solenoid valves, smart positioners, and partial stroke test (PST) instrumentation prior to factory acceptance.
